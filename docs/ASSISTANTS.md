# Vision and Wanda

Two assistants you can talk to directly, beside the seven social media specialists. They help with social media
work and with general questions; more features will be added over time.

| | Vision | Wanda |
|---|---|---|
| Personality | Calm, analytical, precise | Warm, expressive, creative |
| Social media | Strategy, analytics, account health | Posts, captions, campaigns, replies |
| General | Technical questions, projects, planning | Writing, research, everyday tasks |
| Colour and voice | Cyan; a calm masculine voice | Rose; a warm feminine voice |

Both have the same tools and permission limits; only their personality differs. They use the same AI provider
settings as the rest of STARK AI (no extra install).

## How they work

- **Talk to them by name:** "Vision, how did last week's posts do?" or "Hey Wanda, draft three posts about the launch".
- **Drafts, not posts.** They can draft, edit and check posts and read analytics. Publishing still goes through
  D.U.M.-E. and your approval, as before.
- **Calling the specialists.** Either one can hand a job to J.A.R.V.I.S., E.D.I.T.H. and the others, or ask for a
  team review. You first approve which conversation details they may share. If you reject it, nothing is sent.
- **Separate histories.** Vision and Wanda keep their own conversations. To let one continue what the other
  started, write a short handoff in the Command view; only what you write is passed on.
- **Personal notes.** They remember personal things only with your consent. Personal notes are kept apart from
  your brand workspaces and from the other assistant.
- **Presence.** In Command each assistant appears as a living 3D presence: Core (a geodesic sphere), Female or
  Male (your choice, remembered per assistant). Vision is a crisp cyan lattice with golden pulses; Wanda a softer
  rose lattice with streams of energy and a circling aura. It brightens while listening, a band of light scans
  while thinking, and the jaw moves while speaking. The figures are synthetic humans (CC0 base models), not any
  real person; with reduced motion turned on in your system they stay still.
- **Full screen.** Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Space opens a full-screen view: the presence, a live waveform, the latest reply and
  a big talk button (hold Space). Esc closes it.
- **Remembering conversations.** Each assistant can look back through its own past conversations
  (memory.recall), by meaning with your Gemini key or by words without it. Never the other assistant's; nothing
  when memory is turned off.
- **Shortcut and computer control.** ⌥/Alt+Shift+Space opens the assistant from any app. With computer control
  turned on in Settings, they can list an app's buttons, click, type and press shortcuts, each action approved
  first. See [COMPUTER.md](COMPUTER.md).
- **Your computer (desktop app).** Ask "Vision, how's my battery?" or "Wanda, remind me at 6 to post the reel":
  they can check the computer's status, open a web page, a workspace file or an app, show notifications, set
  reminders and use the clipboard. Opening things and the clipboard always ask you first; they never control the
  mouse or keyboard, run commands or change settings.
- **Voices.** With STARK voice (your own free Gemini key) you hold the talk button and speak English or Urdu;
  each assistant answers in their own voice. Without a key, the voices installed on your computer are used.
  Details: [VOICE.md](VOICE.md).

The seven specialists, their roles and their safety limits are unchanged.
