# STARK AI 1.13.0

**Talk to Vision and Wanda.** Hold the talk button, speak English or Urdu, and hear the answer in the assistant's own voice.

## New
- **STARK voice.** Uses your own free Google Gemini key (Settings → STARK voice). Vision speaks in a calm, clear voice; Wanda in a warm, lively one. Start with a name ("Vision…", "وانڈا…") to choose who answers.
- **English and Urdu.** What you say is written in Urdu script or Roman Urdu (your choice), and the assistants reply in the language you speak.
- **Works in the desktop app window.** The Mac and Windows apps use your computer's microphone and speakers directly; nothing extra to install. On a Mac, allow microphone access the first time.
- **Pick the voice you like.** 30 voices and 6 speaking styles for each assistant in Settings → STARK voice, with a Hear button.
- **Fast replies.** The voice is streamed, so speaking starts before the whole reply is ready. If Gemini is busy or over the free limit, your computer's own voice answers instead.

- **A living presence.** In Command, each assistant appears in 3D: Core, Female or Male (your choice, remembered). Vision is a crisp cyan lattice with golden pulses; Wanda a rose lattice with streams of energy. It brightens while listening, scans while thinking and moves as it speaks.
- **Full-screen assistant.** Press Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Space (or the expand button) for a large presence with a live waveform, the latest reply and a big talk button; hold Space to talk, Esc to leave. In the desktop app the presence moves with the real loudness of your voice and theirs.
- **They remember your conversations.** Ask "Vision, what did we decide about the launch?": each assistant can look back through its own past conversations, by meaning when your Gemini key is set (by words otherwise). Turning memory off stops it.
- **Urdu in Nastaliq.** Urdu text uses the traditional Nastaliq style and flows right to left.
- **Help on your computer** (desktop app): "how's my battery?", "open my launch plan", "remind me at 6 to post the reel". Opening things and the clipboard always ask first.

## Safety
- Voice never approves anything. When an action needs approval, the assistant says so and the approval waits on screen.
- Nothing is recorded unless you hold the button; recordings stop after 60 seconds. Your key is stored in the Keychain / Credential Manager.
- Without a Gemini key, voice keeps using your computer's own speech as before.

Installers are not signed yet: on a Mac right-click → **Open** the first time; on Windows choose **More info → Run anyway**.
