# STARK AI 2.2.0

## Dependable voice, one-click sign-in and a new look

### Voice
- **Push-to-talk no longer leaves the microphone on.** Letting go before the microphone opened, a quick tap on a touchscreen (which fires touch and mouse events), switching windows while holding, or pressing twice could leave it recording. Presses and releases are now handled in order, both in the dashboard and in the engine.
- **A microphone plugged in after STARK AI started is found** (USB microphones, Bluetooth headsets).
- **Clear microphone errors:** blocked, not found, or busy in another app, instead of raw browser error names.
- **Recording works in Safari/WebKit** (it picks a format the browser supports) with echo cancellation and noise suppression.
- **Offline fallback.** When Gemini can't answer (no key, no internet, free limit, timeout), speech-to-text uses the on-device speech model if one is downloaded in the Video studio, and replies are read by the computer's own voice instead of failing.
- **Voice check** in Settings → STARK voice: shows the microphone and speaker in use, other microphones, whether offline speech is ready, and listens for 2 seconds to confirm the microphone hears you. Nothing is kept.
- **"Save and restart" works without a Gemini key** when Browser speech is chosen.
- Replies that a browser refuses to play explain that a click is needed first.

### Social accounts
- **Sign in with Mastodon on any server**, no token copying: STARK AI registers itself on your server, your browser opens, you allow it, done. Pasting a token still works.
- **Sign-in pages open in your normal browser** in the desktop app (saved passwords, passkeys and two-factor apps work).
- **No more "I've logged in" button:** STARK AI notices the finished sign-in by itself, and the browser tab closes on its own where the browser allows it.
- **STARK Connect hardening:** it refuses to run without a real `STATE_KEY` (32+ characters), compares signatures in constant time and rejects malformed sign-in states. **Self-hosters: check your `STATE_KEY` is set before updating.**

### Design
- New design system across every screen: deeper surfaces, glass panels with a fine top highlight, refined buttons, inputs, focus rings and display type.
- The sidebar is grouped (Overview, Create, Grow, Automate, Team, System) and stays in view while scrolling.
- Toasts animate in; reduced motion, the Effects: off setting, high contrast and the light theme are respected.

### Not in this release
- Bluesky sign-in through OAuth (the app-password connection is unchanged) and several accounts per platform.
