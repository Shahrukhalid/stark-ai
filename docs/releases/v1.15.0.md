# STARK AI 1.15.0

## New
- **Routines by voice.** "Wanda, every Monday at 9 draft three posts" or "Vision, every morning check yesterday's numbers" becomes a routine after you approve it. Routines show under Automations and never publish on their own.
- **Daily summary.** At a time you pick, Vision or Wanda tells you (in English or Urdu) what is scheduled today, what went out yesterday, what failed, what waits for your approval, which accounts need a look and today's reminders. Settings → Routines and quiet hours.
- **Quiet hours.** No pop-ups or spoken summaries at night; reminders wait until the quiet time ends.
- **Fast mode.** Near-instant spoken replies with Gemini's live voice (Settings → STARK voice → Fast mode, desktop app). Anything real still goes through the assistant, so approvals and E-STOP apply.

Installers are not notarized: on a Mac right-click → **Open** the first time; on Windows choose **More info → Run anyway**.
